Frequently asked questions

Shuru karne se pehle jo poochna zaroori hai.

Transparent answers, taake aapko support ka scope pehle hi clear ho.

Kya aap mere quizzes, assignments ya GDB mere liye complete karenge?

Nahi. Hum assessed work aapki taraf se complete ya submit nahi karte. Hum question, rubric, concepts, approach aur structure samjhate hain, taake aap apna response khud tayar kar saken.

Fees kaise maloom hogi?

Fee course, support type aur required duration ke mutabiq hoti hai. Apna course code aur need WhatsApp par share karein; requirement samajh kar price bataya jayega.

Kya timing flexible hai, khas taur par job holders ke liye?

Ji, job holders aur busy students ke schedule ko madde nazar rakh kar available timing coordinate ki jati hai. Apne preferred days aur time WhatsApp par bhej dein.

Kaun se subjects cover hote hain?

Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT, Management, Mathematics, English aur kai general VU subjects cover kiye jate hain. Exact availability ke liye course code share karein.

EduNexus se is service ka kya rishta hai?

VU Study Support, EduNexus ki learning-focused initiative hai. Free study files, notes aur VU resources ke liye EduNexus study library visit karein.

Main kaise shuru karun?

WhatsApp par apna course code, topic, current difficulty aur preferred timing bhej dein. Aapki requirement dekh kar next suitable step suggest kiya jayega.
